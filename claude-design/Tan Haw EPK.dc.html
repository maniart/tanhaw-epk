Bio

Tan Haw is the stage name of Mani Nilchiani, an Iranian musician who splits his time between Brooklyn and Mexico, trained in Setâr and the Radif tradition, moving between Persian classical roots, psych-rock, and electronica across Persian, Azerbaijani, English, and Spanish.

Tan Haw is the stage name of Mani Nilchiani, an Iranian musician splitting his time between Brooklyn and Mexico, trained in Setâr and the Radif tradition under master musician Fared Shafinury since 2018, with vocal technique under Maziar Sharifian.

His debut LP, Royâ-ye Mahtâb (Soñar con la luna), opens with “Ayriliq” — a departure point rooted in his grandfather's Baku — and traces an intergenerational migration arc from Azerbaijan to Tehran to New York to Mexico, where months spent each year have shaped the record's sound and spirit.

The record moves through Iranian classical structure, microtonal fretless-guitar psych-rock, electronica, and cumbia, sung across Persian, Azerbaijani, English, and Spanish. Tan Haw's work has been featured in Bandcamp Daily's “Big Ups.”
